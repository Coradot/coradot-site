From signed agreement to closing, in five steps

1. Open a Stream

One Stream per side of the deal, opened from the signed listing or purchase agreement.

2. Use approved forms

Only the current versions your brokerage approved, with a checklist that follows the state, transaction type and property type.

3. Sign in one place

Signatures are built in and tied to the file, with every signer and the order they signed in recorded.

4. Broker review

Approve, flag or hold at the checkpoints that matter, while the deal is moving.

5. Close with a full record

Executed documents are locked, every approval and action is attributed, and each file ends with a completion certificate.

Questions brokers ask

Is Coradot a CRM or an e-signature tool?

Neither alone. Coradot runs the full transaction, from the signed agreement to closing, with e-signatures built in.

Can agents change our forms?

No. Brokers and form owners control which forms and which versions are available.

What does the broker control?

Which forms and versions agents can use, who reviews what, and when a file can move. The control starts when a Stream opens: a purchase Stream opens only from a buyer representation agreement the broker has approved.

How is Coradot priced?

Billing is to the brokerage: a monthly platform base set by the size of the brokerage, $49 per agent seat and $49 per completed Stream. Agents are never billed. See the pricing page for the plans.

How does onboarding work?

We set up your brokerage, approved forms, roles and review points, then add your agents.

Your first 3 completed Streams are on us.

Every new brokerage on a Coradot license has the completed-Stream fee waived on its first 3 Streams. See pricing
