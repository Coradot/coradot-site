E-Signatures in Real Estate: What ESIGN and UETA Mean for Your Brokerage

Coradot Team · October 27, 2026

Most real estate paperwork can be signed electronically. Two laws explain why, and what a brokerage should do to keep the record clean.

The two laws

The federal ESIGN Act and the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act (UETA), which most states have adopted, give electronic signatures and records the same legal effect as handwritten ones. The signature must be tied to a person who intended to sign, and the parties must have agreed to do business electronically.

What that means in practice

  • Consent. Each signer agrees to sign electronically before they do.
  • Intent. The record shows each signer acted on purpose, in their own right.
  • The executed copy. The signed document is preserved and can be retrieved later.
  • Who signed, and when. The signing history stays attached to the file.

A few documents are carved out

Some categories, such as wills, court documents and certain default, foreclosure and eviction notices, are not covered by ESIGN. Most listing and purchase paperwork is.
