Pricing.

Every charge is billed to the brokerage on one account. Agents are never billed, and Coradot never deducts from a commission.

Your first 3 completed Streams are on us.

Each new brokerage on a Coradot license has the $49 completed-Stream fee waived on its first 3 completed Streams. Use them within 60 days of approval. The platform base and agent seats bill as normal.

How it adds up

Platform base

A monthly amount set by the size of your brokerage.

$49 per agent seat

Per enabled agent, per month, on every plan.

$49 per completed Stream

For each buyer-side or seller-side Stream that is successfully completed.

Signature included

A pooled monthly allowance on every plan. $1.50 for each envelope beyond it.

An example

An Essential brokerage with 20 agents and 6 completed sides in a month: $799 base + 20 × $49 seats ($980) + 6 × $49 ($294) = $2,073. In its launch month, 3 of those sides are waived, so it is $1,926.
