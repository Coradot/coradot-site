Good questions

Is Coradot a CRM or an e-signature tool?

Neither alone. It runs the full transaction, with signatures built in.

Does Cora give legal advice?

No. She finds missing items, surfaces deadlines and explains status. She never changes signed documents.

Can agents change our forms?

No. Brokers and form owners control which forms are available.

How does onboarding work?

We set up your brokerage, approved forms, roles and review points, then add your agents.
