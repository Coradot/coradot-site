The gap

A transaction lives in too many places.

Forms in one portal, signatures in another, deadlines in a spreadsheet, status in someone's inbox. Brokers are responsible for all of it, but often see it last.

Oversight after the fact

    FAQ

    Common questions

    Is Coradot a CRM or an e-signature tool?+

    Neither on its own. Coradot runs the transaction itself, from signed agreement to close. Signatures are built in through Coradot Signature, so documents, signers and the executed copy stay with the file.

    Does Cora give legal advice?+

    No. Cora helps find missing items, surface deadlines and explain where a file stands. She works from your approved forms and policies, never changes a signed document, and tells you when she can't verify something.

    Can agents change our forms?+

    No. Brokers and form owners control which forms and versions are available. Agents can upload their own documents for signature, but an upload is never treated as an approved form.

    Who can see a transaction?+

    Only the people whose role gives them access, such as the agent, their broker and invited participants. Form owners manage forms but don't get access to private transaction files.

    How does onboarding work?+

    We set up your brokerage, approved forms, roles and review points with you, then bring agents on. We'll go through the details on your demo.
