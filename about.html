About Coradot.

Coradot is the agreement-to-close operating system for brokerages. We build one governed workspace where a transaction runs from the signed agreement to the closing table.

What we believe

The broker is in charge

Brokers decide which forms are used, who reviews what and when a file can move. The software enforces it.

The file is the truth

Status, signatures, deadlines and approvals live in the transaction itself, not in inboxes.

Records that hold up

Executed documents are locked, every action is attributable and every file ends with a complete record.

Plain pricing

One bill to the brokerage. Agents are never billed and commissions are never touched.
